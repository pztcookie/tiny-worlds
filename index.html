tiny worlds

One room in a universe, not an entire universe. Each of these is a small world built in about an hour: it gives you something to want, misbehaves a little, keeps one secret, remembers what you did, and hands you something to keep.

  • Moonlit Sandwich Cafe Compose a sandwich where every part of you gets a place on the plate. self-authorship
  • The Little Monster in the Room Soothe a temperamental cookie, then photograph the room you wish were true. want & undercurrent
