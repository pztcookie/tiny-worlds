# The Little Monster in the Room

A cozy browser mini-game about soothing a temperamental cookie companion long enough to
frame the room you wish were true — and noticing what your hands already know.

## Play

**[Play it in your browser →](https://pztcookie.github.io/tiny-worlds/games/little-monster-room/)**

No build step, no API key, no backend. To run it locally instead:

```bash
cd games/little-monster-room
python3 -m http.server 8765   # then open http://localhost:8765
```

## The loop

1. Enter a pastel room with a chocolate-chip cookie creature that starts **restless**.
2. Watch the temper meter: restless → fidgety → softening → settled.
3. Coax it calm — soft pets, gifts, quiet waiting, gentle tidying, or giving it space.
4. Dragging or forcing the cookie usually raises its temper and invites mischief.
5. When it settles, photograph the **whole room** you wish were true.
6. Optionally zoom into a cookie character close-up.
7. Keep playing for secret journal notes and unlockable decor.
8. Open the personality summary: what you framed (want) vs how you soothed and played (undercurrent).

## Things the world does on its own

While restless, the cookie may:

- scatter objects around the room
- jump to a new spot
- resist being hauled
- change mood without asking

Mystery crumbs, star twinkles and soft rewards appear for curious hands.

## The secret

Stillness is earned, not ordered. The camera only keeps a settled keepsake — and the path
you took to calm the temper becomes part of the reflection.

## Traces

- Temper meter and mood expressions
- Atmosphere shifts from how you interact
- Journal notes (including secret notes)
- Unlockable decor keepsakes you can rearrange
- A dual summary: photograph-as-wish, play-as-pattern
