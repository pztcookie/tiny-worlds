# Brief: The Little Monster in the Room

## 1. Personal lens

*How we coax unrest into stillness — and what that reveals about what we want vs what we
do by habit — without naming the player.*

> Softness, space, order, gifts and patience as different dialects of care; the photograph
> holds a wish, the play holds a pattern.

## 2. Object / theme

- **Environment:** a cozy pastel bedroom with star-flecked walls
- **Main object / character / system:** a temperamental chocolate-chip cookie companion
- **Emotional premise:** you want an ideal still moment, but the living thing will not pose
  until you find a way to soothe it
